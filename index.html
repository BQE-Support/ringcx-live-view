Methodology & notes
  • Data comes from RingCX's real-time supervisor API (realTimeData/agent and realTimeData/inbound), the same source as the RingCX wallboards. It is read with your own RingCentral sign-in, so you only see what your RingCX permissions allow.
  • Status colors follow the RingCX agent app. Green means the status takes calls (Available, Payroll Support). Grey is On-Break, Away and Lunch. Yellow (Busy) is every Working-type status (Training, Testing, Meeting, ticket and escalation work, ePayment, Yodlee and similar). Red means on a call or in wrap-up.
  • SLA is RingCX's own service-level count for each queue, from its real-time data (slaPass / slaFail). A call passes if it is answered within the queue's SLA time, which is 30 seconds on every BQE queue. Calls answered later, or abandoned after 30 seconds, fail. The top tile adds up all queues. The counts are today's running totals.
  • Queue hours: the viewer shows a queue as Open only if RingCX says it's open and, for queues with configured hours, the current time is inside them. Payroll Specialist is open M–F 6am–4pm Pacific, which matches its RingCX schedule. Outside those hours it shows Closed and raises no staffing alerts. If RingCX ever reports it open outside those hours, the page flags it as a schedule problem.
  • "Not taking calls" means any status other than green that isn't a live call or wrap-up. The tiles split this into Busy (yellow statuses) and Break / lunch (grey: On-Break, Away, Lunch).
  • Supervisors (RingCX agent type SUPERVISOR) are listed separately. They move into the Agents table, and into the watch, whenever they are Available or Engaged. Victor Guadarrama is treated as a supervisor too (TREAT_AS_SUPERVISOR in the page settings).
  • The avoidance watch flags an agent only when at least of these signals are true at the same time. One signal alone is not flagged.
    • Any RNA (ring, no answer) today. Two or more is critical.
    • Leaving Available for a status that doesn't take calls + times in the last 60 minutes, or + status changes in 60 minutes.
    • Being in a status that doesn't take calls for longer than . Longer than is critical.
    • Wrap-up longer than .
  • Performance today scores people on a fixed 100-point scorecard (Fred, 2026-10-16). Agents can't control how many calls they're offered, so calls and talk time are shown but don't score:
    • Ready, 30: share of logged-in time Available, Engaged or in Transition. Full at 85% or more, none at 50% or less, in proportion between. Full points until 30 minutes have been watched.
    • No RNA, 15: 1 RNA = 7, 2 or more = 0.
    • On-time start, 12: a confirmed first login 5-15 min after the roster start = 6, later = 0. Unconfirmed first logins count as on time.
    • Lunch within the hour, 8: 5-15 min over = 4, more = 0.
    • Tickets, 15: tickets waiting on them (Zendesk assigned and Open, or On Hold with no due date or a passed one; ES "Awaiting info" or "Resolved to L1") must be handled by the end of their first shift that starts after the ticket got into that state. Each one still waiting after its deadline costs 5; still waiting 2+ days past it costs 10. The server checks every 10 minutes. Click the Tickets cell for the list with links.
    Grades: 90+ green, 75-89 normal, 60-74 yellow, under 60 red. Hover a score for its breakdown. Top 5 and Bottom 5 rank agents only. Supervisors (including Victor Guadarrama) are scored too, but they appear only in "Show all", with a Supervisor tag. Calls answered and talk time come from RingCX's call report for the whole day, refreshed every 5 minutes. Logged-in time, RNA and Available/Engaged time come from RingCX's live data, which only covers each person's current login session. This browser adds them up across logins, but only for what it saw while open. Open the page at the start of the day for the most accurate ranking. People who logged out before the page started tracking can't be ranked, because their logged-in time is unknown. Supervisors who answered queue calls are included.
  • "WORKING" on its own (not a WORKING sub-status like Escalating to L3) is the one status agents can't choose themselves in RingCX. RingCX puts people there itself, for example after a temporary disconnect, and it blocks calls. Anyone in it gets a "Lost Connection" tag next to their status. After they go on the Call avoidance watch on their own (no second signal needed), and the flag turns red after , so a supervisor can ask them to update their status.
  • Ticket work with no trace: anyone in ZD/ES Ticket Follow-up or Testing for longer than with nothing in Zendesk or Jira since the status started (no reply, note, status or assignee change, Jira comment or time logged) goes on the Call avoidance watch on their own. It turns red after . The check is the same as the Activity dropdown in the status list and repeats every 2 minutes, so the flag clears as soon as something shows up. Just reading tickets leaves no trace, so ask before assuming.
  • Vendor status in What to Watch comes from each vendor's public status page, checked every 2 minutes: Gusto (the API component only), GlobalPay (Worldpay, plus Payrix US), and BQE CORE (both regions). It shows a degraded or down component, an open incident, or maintenance in progress. Red = major outage or a major/critical incident; yellow = anything else. Click it to open the status page. Global Payments has no public developer status page we could find, so GlobalPay covers Worldpay and Payrix.
  • Longest wait is the caller who has waited longest in any queue right now. Its tile flashes red once that wait passes , and keeps flashing until no caller has waited that long, meaning they were answered or hung up. Queue cards with such a caller flash too.
  • Status changes are counted by this browser while the page is open. RingCX's real-time API only reports each agent's current status, so the page records every change it sees between refreshes. History is kept in this browser for the current day. If an agent changes status and comes back between two refreshes, the page still catches it from the reset status timer, but it counts as one change. A 15-second refresh catches more. Changes from before you opened the page are not included. Check the "watching since" time.
  • Calls, talk time, RNA and logged-in time are today's totals everywhere on the page, so the Agents table and Performance always agree. RingCX's live counters reset each time someone logs in again, so this page adds up every login session it saw today, and also uses the day's call report for calls and talk. Login sessions from before the page started tracking can't be counted. Queue counters (presented, answered, abandoned) are RingCX's daily running totals. Abandoned includes very short transfer legs (≤5s), so it overstates real lost callers.
  • The page makes two requests per refresh and pauses while the window is hidden. RingCX allows about 120 real-time requests per minute in total.
  • This view is read-only. It cannot change agent states or take calls. Keep the normal RingCX agent window open for that.
  • The page checks for a newer published version every 5 minutes, and whenever you come back to the window. If there is one, it reloads itself. You stay signed in and keep your settings and history.
